## 🌟 Overview

ViralPro LK provides all the digital services a modern business needs under one roof:
- **Promotional Videos & Reels:** Shot with iPhone 16 Pro Max 4K ProRes and professional stabilizers, optimized for Facebook Reels, TikTok, and Instagram (9:16 vertical).
- **Social Media Management:** Facebook, Instagram, TikTok & YouTube strategy, content planning, and Meta advertising.
- **Graphic Design & Branding:** Logos, flyers, banners, business cards, menu cards, and packaging.
- **Web & Software Development:** Business websites, landing pages, e-commerce, web applications, and mobile apps.
- **Business Systems & POS:** Point of Sale (POS) billing, inventory control, and real-time business analytics dashboards.
- **Automation & AI:** AI chatbots, WhatsApp business automation, and workflow integration.
- **Education & LMS Platforms:** Online class platforms, course management, student databases, and payment workflows.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans & Noto Sans Sinhala
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas (Inquiry and lead capture pipeline)
- **Deployment:** Vercel

## ⚙️ Configuration & Content Management

All service items, pricing labels, package features, FAQ answers, team roles, and contact credentials can be updated from **`src/config/siteConfig.ts`** without touching frontend UI components.
